# Finance Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Title

**Finance Performance Dashboard**

## 📖 Short Description

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze revenue, profit, customer segments, payment methods, and regional financial performance. The dashboard helps businesses monitor key financial metrics, evaluate profitability trends, and identify high-performing cities and regions for strategic decision-making.

---

## 🚩 Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to track revenue, profit, customer segments, and payment preferences across multiple regions and cities. Without a centralized financial reporting system, identifying profitable areas, monitoring performance against targets, and understanding customer behavior becomes difficult.

This dashboard helps stakeholders:

* Monitor revenue and profit performance.
* Compare profitability across regions and cities.
* Analyze customer segments and payment methods.
* Track financial targets and achievements.
* Support strategic financial planning.

## 💡 Key Insights

### Revenue Insights

* Total Revenue reached approximately **6 Billion**, indicating strong business performance.
* UPI generated the highest revenue contribution among all payment methods.
* Cash, Credit Card, and Net Banking contributed almost equally to total revenue.

### Profitability Insights

* Maximum Profit recorded was approximately **90K**.
* Minimum Profit recorded was approximately **-50K**, highlighting potential loss-making transactions.
* Quarterly profit remained relatively stable with a slight increase in Q3 and Q4.

### Regional Insights

* North Region contributed the highest share of overall profit.
* South Region ranked second in profitability.
* East and West regions showed comparatively lower profit contributions.

### City Performance Insights

* Delhi generated the highest revenue among all cities.
* Kolkata closely followed Delhi in revenue contribution.
* Mumbai, Pune, Chennai, and Bangalore displayed consistent revenue performance.
* Waterfall analysis shows cumulative profit growth across all major cities.

### Customer Insights

* Corporate and Individual customer segments can be analyzed separately using interactive filters.
* Product-wise filtering enables detailed profitability and revenue analysis.

### Strategic Insights

* Promote UPI transactions as they contribute the highest revenue.
* Focus on high-performing cities such as Delhi and Kolkata for business expansion.
* Investigate transactions causing negative profits.
* Strengthen lower-performing regions to improve overall profitability.
* Monitor revenue targets regularly to improve financial performance.
